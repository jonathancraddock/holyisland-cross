How it works

1

Northumberland County Council publishes the times when the causeway is safe. I've taken that data and found the midpoint of every safe window.

2

Set off 90 to 120 minutes before that midpoint and the tide is still falling all the way across — you'll be off the sands well before it turns.

3

I only show crossings that fall in daylight. The Pilgrim's Way is three miles of open sand and mud with no lighting, and should only be crossed during the daytime.

4

The causeway (the tarmac road) is open for longer than the sands are safe to walk, so I show both. Handy if you're leaving one car on the island and driving the other back to the mainland before walking.
